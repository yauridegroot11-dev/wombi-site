Help build the
Wombi recorder.

Wombi is a small recorder you press to catch a thought. It sends the recording to your phone, and the app turns it into notes, tasks and plans. The app works today. The recorder is a working prototype on a Seeed XIAO nRF52840 Sense, and I am looking for an intern to help take it from prototype to first run.

Real hardware, real firmware, shipped to real people.

The work

You would work on

  • Firmware for the button, the mic and recording
  • The Bluetooth link to the phone: no recording ever lost, the owner lock, battery
  • Fitting the buzz motor, the speaker and the recording light
  • Battery life, the board and the case, toward a first run

You might be a fit if

  • You have built something with a microcontroller, and can show it
  • You write C or C++, or are learning it fast
  • A soldering iron and a multimeter do not scare you
  • You like finding out why something broke
